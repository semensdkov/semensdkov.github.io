How We Make the Magic

Our innovative 3-step process ensures that nothing goes to waste. What used to clog our pipes now cleans our homes.

1. You Collect

Instead of pouring used cooking oil down the drain, simply store it in a sealed container. We handle the rest.

2. We Convert

Through a safe, green chemical process called saponification, we transform the waste oil into rich, cleaning soap bases.

3. You Clean

The result? A premium, biodegradable detergent that cuts through grease effortlessly without harming the environment.
